# CuteCute Rampage — Depth Pass 02

This pass turns the first combat scaffold into a build-driven run with distinct weapon roles, risk/reward momentum, elites, a boss milestone and an evolution reward loop.

## Build identity

Taffi now has three weapon families that can coexist:

- **Heart Blaster** — the reliable forward auto-fire. Heart upgrades raise mastery as well as raw damage/projectile count.
- **Cupcake Mortar** — slow automatic lob that targets priority enemies and explodes in an area.
- **Love Orbit** — persistent close-range orbiting hearts that reward threading through crowds.

Level-up choices can unlock and improve Cupcake Mortar and Love Orbit. The HUD shows the current weapon loadout and levels.

## Evolution chests

The first boss drops a legendary evolution chest. It checks the current run build and evolves an eligible weapon:

- Heart Blaster → **HEARTSTORM**: faster, larger heart shots with additional projectile and multi-target pierce.
- Cupcake Mortar → **BIRTHDAY MASSACRE**: larger/faster cupcake blasts followed by a radial ring of piercing sugar-stars.
- Love Orbit → **HALO OF HUGS**: seven larger orbiting hearts with higher damage and radius.

If no weapon currently qualifies, the legendary chest unlocks a missing secondary weapon rather than giving a dead reward. Once the build is complete, it becomes a large all-round stat cache.

Elite enemies have a small chance to drop normal reward chests for additional mid-run spikes.

## Cute Meter

`CUTE` is now a 0–100 momentum resource separate from combo.

- kills raise it faster as the combo grows;
- Perfect Dodge gives a large bump;
- taking damage removes a large chunk and cuts combo;
- after a short grace period it slowly decays;
- higher Cute Meter directly increases player weapon damage and XP value.

Ranks are SWEET → CUTE → KAWAII → SUGAR RUSH → STRAWBERRY FEVER. Screen sparkles, pink atmosphere and corner hearts intensify with the meter so successful play literally makes the massacre prettier.

## Elite affixes

Elite enemies now roll one of three modifiers:

- **Swift** — much faster, slightly less HP;
- **Tank** — larger, slower, far more HP and stronger contact damage;
- **Volatile** — dangerous contact profile and fires a radial candy burst when killed.

This adds threat recognition beyond enemy species/archetype.

## First boss: Queen Mallow

At roughly 2:30 the director introduces the first boss milestone, **QUEEN MALLOW**.

She has two health phases and alternates between:

- orbiting/spacing movement;
- radial candy barrages;
- aimed fan bullets in phase two;
- telegraphed high-speed body charges.

Normal horde density is reduced while the boss is alive so the encounter stays readable rather than becoming pure soup. The HUD exposes a boss name/health bar. Killing Queen Mallow produces exaggerated gore, fills momentum resources and drops the evolution chest.
